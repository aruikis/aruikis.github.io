What Vault X is

Vault X is a personal collection tracker for trading-card games, comics, LEGO sets, Funko Pop figures, and other collectibles. It lets you catalog what you own, see current market values, and — only if you choose — share your collection with friends inside the app.

Information we collect

Vault X collects only what the app needs to work:

  • Account information (optional). If you create an account or sign in, we store your email address and a password credential. Sign-in is handled by Supabase Auth; your password is stored by the authentication service in hashed form and is never visible to us in plain text. Signing in is optional for browsing your own on-device collection and is used to protect and sync your saved data.
  • Your collection data. The items you add to your collection (cards and collectibles you own, quantities, prices you paid, decks, watchlists, and similar entries you create) are stored in our database, hosted by Supabase, so they are backed up and available when you sign in.
  • Content you share in the app. If you use the app's social features (friends, mail, forums, trades), the content you send through those features is stored so it can be delivered to the people you send it to. Other users can see your collection only if you friend them inside the app and sharing is turned on.

What we do NOT collect

  • No advertising. Vault X contains no ads and no advertising SDKs.
  • No third-party trackers. Vault X contains no analytics or tracking SDKs from third parties.
  • No data sales. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to anyone.
  • No location, contacts, or device data. Vault X does not collect your location, address book, call history, or browsing history.

How your information is used

Your information is used only to operate the app: to sign you in, to store and display your own collection, to fetch current market prices for items, and to deliver the social features you choose to use. We do not use your information for advertising or profiling.

Data storage and security

Account and collection data are stored with Supabase (a hosted Postgres database provider). Access to saved collection tables is restricted by row-level security rules so that a signed-in user can access only their own data. Data in transit is encrypted with HTTPS.

Data retention and deletion

Your collection data is kept while your account exists. If you want your account and associated data deleted, contact us at aruikis@gmail.com and we will delete them.

Children

Vault X is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy
