Vault X — Account and Data Deletion

App: Vault X
Developer: Anthony Ruikis
Contact: aruikis@gmail.com

How to request deletion of your account

To delete your Vault X account and all of its associated data, follow these steps:

  1. Send an email to aruikis@gmail.com from the email address used on your Vault X account.
  2. Use the subject line: Delete my Vault X account
  3. Include your Vault X username in the message so we can find your account.

We process deletion requests within 30 days and confirm by email when the deletion is complete.

What gets deleted

  • Your account: email address, username, and sign-in credential.
  • Your collection data: items, quantities, prices paid, decks, and watchlists you saved.
  • Your social content: forum posts, in-app mail, and trade offers you sent or received.
  • Your friend connections inside the app.

No data is kept after an account deletion, other than records we are legally required to retain; Vault X normally has none. Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

How to request deletion of some data only

You can also ask for specific data to be deleted — for example your collection, or your forum posts — without deleting your account. Email aruikis@gmail.com from the email address on your account, say which data you want removed, and we will delete it within 30 days and confirm by email.
