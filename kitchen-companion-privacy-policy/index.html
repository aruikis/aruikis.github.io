Kitchen Companion — Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 20, 2026
Developer: Anthony Ruikis
Contact: aruikis@gmail.com

What Kitchen Companion is

Kitchen Companion is a personal recipe library. It lets you save recipes you find — from a link, a photo of a recipe page, a file, or typed in by hand — and keep them organized, searchable, and easy to cook from. You can also plan meals, build a grocery list, and, only if you choose, share recipes with friends and family inside the app.

Information we collect

Kitchen Companion collects only what the app needs to work:

  • Your recipes and photos. The recipes you save, the photos you attach to them (a finished dish, or the original page you scanned), and your meal plans and grocery lists are stored on your device, in your own library. They are yours; the app does not send them anywhere except as described below.
  • Your cook profile (optional). If you use the friends features, you pick a username and display name and may add a profile photo and a few details about your cooking. That profile, together with your friend code, is stored in our database, hosted by Supabase, so friends who have your code can find you. There is no email address and no password involved — your profile is identified by your username and friend code.
  • Recipes you choose to share. Nothing is shared automatically. When you tap to share a recipe or a collection with friends, that content is stored in our database so it can be delivered to the friends you picked. Friends see only what you shared with them.
  • Optional backup copy. The app can keep a backup of your library on your device automatically. Separately, and only if you turn it on, it can keep a copy in our database so you can recover your library on a new phone. That server copy is off unless you turn it on, and recovering it requires your private backup key, which is never shared with friends — only a scrambled (hashed) check of it is stored.
  • Links you paste. When you add a recipe from a link, the app fetches that page to read the recipe from it. It fetches only the exact link you paste.

What we do NOT collect

  • No advertising. Kitchen Companion contains no ads and no advertising SDKs.
  • No third-party trackers. Kitchen Companion contains no analytics or tracking SDKs from third parties.
  • No data sales. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to anyone.
  • No location, contacts, or device data. Kitchen Companion does not collect your location, address book, call history, or browsing history. (If you write a location on your public cook profile, that is text you chose to share with friends — the app never reads your device's location.)
  • No grocery accounts. The grocery feature prepares a list you can take to Jewel-Osco's own website. The app never asks for, sees, or stores your grocery-store sign-in; you sign in and pay on the store's site yourself.

How your information is used

Your information is used only to operate the app: to store and display your own recipes, to read recipes from links and photos you provide, and to deliver the sharing and backup features you choose to use. We do not use your information for advertising or profiling.

Data storage and security

Your library lives on your device. Profile, sharing, and optional backup data are stored with Supabase (a hosted Postgres database provider). Data in transit is encrypted with HTTPS.

Data retention and deletion

Recipes on your device are deleted when you delete them or remove the app. If you want your cook profile, shared recipes, and any server backup copy deleted from our database, contact us at aruikis@gmail.com and we will delete them.

Children

Kitchen Companion is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
